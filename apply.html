4Commitment Acknowledgments
This is a Cooper team, not a club. Please read each commitment carefully and check the box only if your student can genuinely fulfill it.
Team meeting schedule: Starting September 29, students should keep Tuesday afternoons clear for recurring Debate Team meetings. Exact meeting time and location are communicated to accepted team members.

Tournaments: Saturday tournaments approximately once per month (5 total). Students must attend at least 3 of 5. No FCPS transportation provided — parents drive or carpool.
5Short Answer Questions
Answer honestly and in your own words. There are no right or wrong answers — the coach wants to hear your voice.
