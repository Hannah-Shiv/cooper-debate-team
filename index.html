What to expect

Debate together in Public Forum

Public Forum is a structured 35-minute back-and-forth with prepared, well-researched speeches, crossfire, and thinking on your feet. Every round takes two debaters working as a team.

  • Partner up. Choose your partner or request a pairing.
  • Prepare beyond practice. Research, write, and practice with your partner outside Tuesday meetings.
  • Collaborate well. Communicate with your partner and contribute to a civil, prepared team.

For students and families

Plan for the season

  • Practices: Tuesdays after school in A and B Sessions.
  • Tournaments: Monthly Saturday tournaments. Parents are responsible for transportation to and from tournaments and may arrange carpools.
  • Fees: $20 per Cooper debater each year covers annual WASDL dues and five preliminary tournaments. Metro Finals or Nationals may add $15–$20 per student.
  • Communication: Coaches use Talking Points and Schoology, not the team app.
  • Watching rounds: Check with the judge and the other debaters before observing; remain quiet and do not signal during the round.
