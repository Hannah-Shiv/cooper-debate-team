Program History

Hall of Fame

A timeline of Cooper Debate's most historic achievements

2025
NSDA Nationals — 4th Place Finish

Cooper Debate competed on the national stage for the first time in program history, finishing 4th at NSDA Nationals — the highest finish any Cooper debate team has ever achieved.

NationalHistoric Best
2025
Metro Finals Champions

Swept the WACFL Metro Finals to claim the regional championship, earning the team's first-ever regional title and a national qualifying bid.

WACFLRegional Champs
2024
WACFL Regular Season Excellence

Finished the regular WACFL season with the best win-loss record in program history, earning top seeding entering the championship rounds.

WACFLBest Record
2023
First Regional Semifinal Appearance

Cooper Debate reached the WACFL semifinal round for the first time, signaling the program's rise to competitive prominence in the Northern Virginia circuit.
