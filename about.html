Who We Are

Our Mission

The Cooper Debate Team develops middle school students into confident, critical thinkers and effective communicators. Through the discipline of Public Forum debate, we equip students with skills that extend far beyond the debate round.

We believe every student has something important to say — and the power to say it well. Our team competes at the highest levels while fostering collaboration, curiosity, and mutual respect.

"The aim of argument, or of discussion, should not be victory, but progress." — Joseph Joubert

What We Stand For

Core Values

The principles that shape every practice, every round, and every debater who wears the Cooper name.

Speak with Clarity

We develop voices that are articulate, persuasive, and grounded in evidence.

Reason with Rigor

Critical thinking, research, and logical argumentation are the heart of what we do.

Lead with Integrity

We compete fiercely and always with sportsmanship, respect, and honesty.

The Benefits

Why Debate?

Debate is one of the most transformative academic activities a student can pursue. The research is clear — debaters outperform peers in critical thinking, academic achievement, and career outcomes.

01
Public Speaking

Overcome stage fright and develop the poise to speak confidently in front of any audience.

02
Critical Thinking

Analyze complex issues, evaluate evidence, and construct logical arguments under pressure.

03
Research Skills

Learn to find, evaluate, and cite credible sources on real-world policy issues.

04
Leadership & Confidence

Build the self-assurance to lead, persuade, and make a difference in any field.

Public Forum Debate

The Format We Compete In

Public Forum (PF) is a two-on-two debate format focused on current events and policy issues. Teams alternate arguing both sides of a resolution — developing intellectual flexibility and a deep understanding of real-world issues.

4
min
Constructive

Each team presents their initial case arguments.

3
min
Crossfire

Both teams question each other's arguments directly.

4
min
Rebuttal

Teams attack the opposing case and defend their own.

2
min
Summary

Each team crystallizes the most important voting issues.

2
min
Final Focus

Last chance to persuade the judge — usually 2-3 key arguments.

Our Circuit

WASDL

The Washington Area Speech and Debate League (WASDL), formerly WACFL, is the premier middle and high school debate circuit in the DC/Northern Virginia region. Cooper Debate competes at WASDL tournaments throughout the fall and spring seasons, culminating in the Metro Finals championship.

Regular Season
★

Multiple WASDL tournaments throughout the year with varsity and junior varsity divisions.

Metro Finals
★

The regional championship — Cooper won it in 2025 to advance to NSDA Nationals.

NSDA Nationals
★

The national championship — we placed 4th, our best finish in program history.

Get Involved

Join Cooper Debate

No experience necessary. We welcome any Cooper Middle School student who is committed, curious, and ready to work hard. Here's how to get started:

1
Attend an Info Session

Come to one of our open practice sessions at the start of the school year to learn what debate is all about.

2
Submit an Application

Fill out the short application form describing why you want to join and what you hope to gain from the experience.

3
Attend Tryouts

Participate in a short practice debate so coaches can assess your current skills and potential.

4
Start Competing

Once accepted, you'll begin practicing and compete in your first WASDL tournament!
