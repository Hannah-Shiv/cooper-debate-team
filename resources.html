Build Your Toolkit

Debate Skills

Case Writing

A strong 4-minute constructive has 2–3 contentions, each with a clear claim, warrant, and impact. Lead with your strongest argument and pre-empt top counterarguments.

"Make your tag line a thesis statement, not a label."
Crossfire

Use crossfire strategically: ask short, closed questions to expose contradictions. Never answer a question you haven't thought through. Control pace — don't let your opponent run the clock.

"Ask questions, don't make speeches."
Summary & Final Focus

In summary, extend 2–3 key issues with offense. Respond to the most damaging attacks. In Final Focus, crystalize 1–2 voting issues and explain why you win them.

"Tell the judge what to weigh and why your evidence is better."
Impact Calculus

Compare arguments using magnitude (how big), probability (how likely), and timeframe (how soon). A clear calculus framing wins close rounds.

"Don't just win an argument — tell the judge why it matters MORE than theirs."
Delivery

Speak conversationally in PF — judges want to understand you, not be impressed by speed. Eye contact, vocal variation, and clear signposting make you a more persuasive speaker.

"If the judge can't follow you, you're losing."

Practice Methods

How We Train

Timed Practice Rounds

We run full practice rounds weekly to build comfort with PF timing. Timers always running — no exceptions.

Flow Practice

Flowing is a critical skill. We practice flowing pre-written speeches until every debater can flow at full speaking speed.

Video Review

We record and review practice rounds to identify speech habits, argument gaps, and crossfire patterns to improve.
